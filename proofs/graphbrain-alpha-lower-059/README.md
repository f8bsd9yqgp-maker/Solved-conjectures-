## Statement
α≥μ−|C|−Δ.

## Disproof
Use the same hub-chain of odd r≥7 copies of K4. Then α=r, μ=2r, the unique center is the middle hub, and Δ=5 (three clique neighbors and two bridges at each interior hub). Thus the right side is 2r−6>r, with witness 7<8 at r=7. ∎
