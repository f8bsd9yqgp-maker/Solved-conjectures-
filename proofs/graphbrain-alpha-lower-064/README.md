## Statement
alpha ≥ min(dd,1/cos(|C|)).

## Disproof
Let T_d be the tree with a single hub and d paths of distinct lengths 1,2,...,d attached to it, with d≥4. Its complement F_d has n=1+d(d+1)/2 vertices and alpha=2 because T_d has clique number two. The hub is the unique vertex of degree d in the tree; the arms have distinct lengths and each is fixed pointwise once the hub is fixed. Thus the automorphism group of T_d, and of its complement, has order one. The complement is connected of diameter two: every tree edge uv has a common nonneighbor in T_d, as deg_T(u)+deg_T(v)≤d+2<n, so every nonadjacent pair of F_d has a common neighbor. No vertex of F_d is universal, since T_d has no isolates. All vertices therefore have eccentricity two.
Use d=4. The graph has n=11, center all eleven vertices, and three distinct degrees (tree degrees 1,2,4). By the rational pi interval, 0<11−7pi/2<1/3, and cos(11)=sin(11−7pi/2) is strictly between zero and 1/3. Thus 1/cos(11)>3, so RHS=3>2.
