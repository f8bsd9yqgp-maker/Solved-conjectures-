## Statement
α ≤ floor(max(|Per|,dd)/density).

## Disproof
For m≥12 take K_{m,m}, choose a vertex h, and attach two paths of length three edges at h, otherwise vertex-disjoint and disjoint from the core. The degree values are 1,2,m,m+2, so dd=4. The diameter is six, attained by the two arm endpoints; core vertices are at distance at most five from any endpoint, and all other vertices have eccentricity at most five. Thus |Per|=2. The edge density is (m²+6)/C(2m+6,2). Choose the m vertices in the core part opposite h and the first and third vertex of each arm. This independent set has size m+4. At m=12 density=10/29, so the claimed bound is floor(58/5)=11, while α≥16. For all m≥12, density≥1/3 because 3(m²+6)−(2m+6)(2m+5)/2=m²−11m+3>0. Therefore the asserted bound is at most twelve, less than m+4. ∎
