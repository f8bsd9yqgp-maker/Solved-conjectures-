## Statement
α(G) ≥ μ(G)−Δ(G)−SW(G).

## Disproof
Take r≥9 copies of K4 in a chain, linked by one bridge between consecutive cliques, with distinct bridge endpoints within interior cliques. Then α=r (one nonbridge vertex per clique), μ=2r (perfect matching within each clique), and Δ=4. Degeneracy is three: every clique supplies minimum degree three, and each nonempty induced subgraph has a vertex of degree at most three. Indeed, if it contains a nonbridge vertex, that vertex has degree at most three. If it consists entirely of bridge endpoints, every vertex has at most one neighbor in its own clique and at most one bridge neighbor, so minimum degree is at most two. The author’s implementation defines SW as degeneracy plus one, so SW=4 and the conjectured lower bound is 2r−8>r for r≥9. At r=9 the right side is ten and α=9. The previous eight-clique example was an equality under the author’s definition and has been replaced. The disproof family remains valid. ∎
