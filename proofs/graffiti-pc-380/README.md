# Graffiti.pc Conjecture 380 — Even-degree vertices and center eccentricity

24 November 2026. AI-assisted written candidate resolution. Independent review, Lean verification, and discovery priority remain unconfirmed. Source: E. DeLaViña, [Written on the Wall II](https://www.uhd.edu/academics/sciences/mathematics-statistics/wowii/files/wowiiaii.pdf); the numbered statements are also available in the [August 2026 transcription](https://github.com/Kuberwastaken/c5-k4/blob/main/corpora/graffiti_pc_wow2.json). No prior complete resolution was identified in targeted searches; this is not an exhaustive priority claim.

Let C be the eccentricity center of T, E the set of even-degree vertices, and r=max_{v∈V}dist(v,C). The November catalogue, printed pp. 223–224, explicitly marks this entry open and defines eccentricity of a set as maximum distance to the set. The conjecture reads t≥|E|/(r−1). We prove it on the defined domain r>1. When r=1 the original quotient has zero denominator and is not a numerical statement.

Let D,U,c,a,b,e_U be as in the following count. Contract the components of T[D]; edge counting gives Σ_{u∈U}(a(u)−1)+e_U=c−1, where a(u),b(u) count neighbors in D,U respectively. Thus Σ_{u∈U}(deg(u)−1)=c−1+e_U≤2(c−1). Every unselected nonleaf contributes at least one. Since c≤floor(t/2), the number of nonleaves is at most t+2(c−1)≤2t−2. Even-degree vertices are nonleaves. Therefore when r≥3, |E|≤2t−2≤(r−1)t.

If r=2, T has diameter four or five, and its center consists of one vertex or a central edge, respectively. Root the tree away from this center (in the two-center case delete the central edge to define the two sides). Every noncentral nonleaf is a support vertex at distance one from the center and is forced into D. Each central vertex not itself a support has a noncentral support neighbor on its own side, since a diameter path extends to depth two on that side. Such a support can be dominated only by its central parent or one of its private leaves. Consequently each nonsupport central vertex requires one extra selected vertex outside the support set; these requirements have disjoint choices for distinct centers. Every central support is already forced into D. It follows that t is at least the number of nonleaves, and hence t≥|E|. This proves the conjecture whenever its denominator is positive. ∎
