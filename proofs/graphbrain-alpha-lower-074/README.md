## Statement
alpha ≥ ceil(log(avg_distance))^kappa.

## Disproof
Take the lexicographic product C15[K2], i.e. replace each cycle vertex by an edge and join all vertices between neighboring clusters. Alpha=7 since at most one per cluster and selected clusters must form a cycle independent set. The graph is 4-connected: deleting fewer than four vertices empties at most one cluster, and the remaining nonempty clusters are connected along a path or cycle. Deleting two nonadjacent clusters shows kappa=4. Average distance is 113/29>3>e: from a root the distance sum to the other 29 vertices is 1+2(2(1+...+7))=113. Hence ceil(log(avg_distance))≥2 and RHS≥16>7.
