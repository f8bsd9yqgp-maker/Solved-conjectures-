# Prior art and attribution

The exact target is Conjecture 1.2 in Akbari, Kumar, Mohar, Pragada and Zhang, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07264. The earlier general n−1 result and DNN method are attributed to Liu, Tang and Zhang, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18031. The quantitative block-deficit method builds on Akbari, Hu and Liu, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06778; their 2-connected result is https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04069. The simplified DNN proof uses Hu and Han, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18492. The unicyclic sign identity is related to Ning and Zeng, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24668. The earlier extremal spectral projection work is Zhang, https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15504. The full manuscript rederives the required DNN and phase statements rather than silently assuming these sources establish the unrestricted result.

The proposed new closing ingredients are the rooted-cluster reduction in a strict minimal counterexample, the uniform apex-unicyclic phase bound, and their combination with the block deficit and phase machinery. The earlier execution performed targeted searches for overlapping solutions in both user-specified GitHub repositories, with no matching solution identified.
