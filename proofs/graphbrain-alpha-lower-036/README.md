## Statement
alpha ≥ min(dd,diam−cut_vertices).

## Disproof
Take cliques of sizes 4,6,8,10 in a chain, with two independent edges between each pair of consecutive cliques. Use the last two vertices of a clique for its outgoing edges and the first two of the next for incoming edges, so in an interior clique incoming and outgoing endpoints are disjoint. No cut vertex exists: deletion of any single vertex preserves connectivity of every remaining clique and at least one link between consecutive cliques. Alpha=4, by partition and choosing vertices not incident to bridge edges in each clique. Degrees are 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10, giving dd=8. Nonbridge vertices in the end cliques are distance seven apart (three cross-clique edges, two intermediate clique hops, and two end hops), so diameter≥7. RHS≥7>4.
