# Solved conjectures

## Pure-state Wigner negativity

Every pure state of finite spin $j>0$ has a self-dual spherical Wigner function that is strictly negative on an open region of the sphere. The manuscript also gives an explicit lower bound on the integrated negative volume at each spin.

[Read the proof](proofs/pure-spin-wigner-negativity/README.md) · [Download the three-page PDF](proofs/pure-spin-wigner-negativity/Pure_Spin_Wigner_Negativity.pdf) · [Open Quantum Problems F18](https://open-quantum-problems.vercel.app/p/F18)

Manuscript dated 18 November 2026. Independent review and publication priority remain unverified.

## Shear-wave band-gap density in periodic laminates

For ideal periodic laminates with rationally independent layer transit times, the proposed proof establishes which sequence maximizes the fraction of frequencies in stop bands at a fixed layer count. The note gives the even- and odd-count results and predictions for aluminum/steel shear-wave filters.

[Read the result and reproduction guide](proofs/shear-wave-band-gap-density/README.md) · [Download the five-page PDF](proofs/shear-wave-band-gap-density/Aluminum_steel_ultrasound.pdf) · [Lustig and Shmuel’s 2018 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmps.2018.04.008)

Public review version dated 20 November 2026. Independent review and publication priority remain open.
